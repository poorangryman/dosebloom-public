DoseBloom is a private Android medication tracker for schedules, reminders, dose history, and medication stock.

Features:
- medication schedules and courses
- today's doses and calendar-based history
- taken and skipped dose tracking
- as-needed medications
- medication stock and low-stock warnings
- multiple user profiles
- scheduled reminders and notification actions
- home-screen widget for the next scheduled dose
- JSON export and import, including intake history
- light and dark themes
- English and Russian languages
- no mandatory account or cloud service
- no advertising

DoseBloom stores application data locally on the device. Exported JSON files may contain sensitive medication information and should be stored securely.

DoseBloom is a personal tracking tool and is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or professional healthcare. Always follow the instructions provided by your doctor or medication packaging.
